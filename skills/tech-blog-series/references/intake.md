# Phase 1 intake

Ask in **one** message (AskUserQuestion takes up to 4 questions per call, so use two calls
at most). Skip anything the conversation already answers. Recommend a default for each
question, and let the user override it.

## A. Always ask

1. **References.** "Which sites or blogs should I learn from? Send your own site or blog
   (for voice and page design), plus 2–4 posts whose style you'd like this to feel like."
2. **Audience.** "Who must this work for?" Default: an engineer new to this domain, a
   decision-maker (buyer or head of a team), and a curious non-engineer.
3. **Purpose.** Teach a concept · tell the story of how we built X · argue a position ·
   recruit or show credibility · launch something.
4. **Scope.** One post, or a series. Default: a series of 3–5 at most, with post 1 as
   context and prerequisites.

## B. Ask only when there's no published writing to copy the voice from

5. **Person and stance.** First-person "I" (practitioner) · "we" (team voice) · neutral
   explainer.
6. **Tone.** Plain and direct · warm storytelling · dry and technical · opinionated.
7. **Shape.** A story-led opening (a scene, then the problem) or a reference-led one (the
   problem, then the answer). How long: about 1,500 · about 2,500 · 4,000+ words.
8. **Devices.** Analogies from everyday life (yes/no) · contractions (yes/no) · one bold
   rule per section (yes/no) · humour (none/light).
9. **Figures.** Few and essential (3–4) · one per idea (5–7) · diagram-heavy.
10. **Never.** Words, phrases or formats they dislike (e.g. em dashes, emoji, "In this
    post", listicles).

Turn the answers into a `VOICE SIGNATURE` in the same format as
`voice-example.md`. Show it to the user in five lines, and offer to save it as
`technical-content-writer/references/personas/<name>.md`.

## C. Ask during phase 2 or 6, not up front

- Which numbers, incidents, customers, production details and prompts may be published
  (clearance).
- Any outcome numbers the decision-maker will want (latency, cost per unit, acceptance
  rate), and whether they're publishable.
- Facts about their own system that reviewers keep questioning. Record the user's answer
  as a fixed input.
